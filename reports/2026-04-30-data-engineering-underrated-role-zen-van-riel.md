## Summary

- Data engineering is the most underrated engineering role in 2026: lower competition, strong pay, and genuinely future-proof.
- 60% of AI projects are expected to be abandoned in 2026 (Gartner, Feb 2025) — the leading root cause is data quality and infrastructure failure.
- Netflix and Spotify case studies show that data engineering infrastructure is what makes AI-powered personalization at scale actually work.
- Three reasons to consider data engineering now: (1) only 2.5 candidates per job posting, (2) avg US salary ~$130K, (3) more AI growth = more demand for data engineers, not less.
- Data engineering is also one of the best entry points into AI engineering, with significant skill overlap.

---

## Key Points

1. **The core argument:** AI solutions are only as good as their data. You can build the most sophisticated agent with the best models, but without clean, structured, accessible business data, it's useless — you might as well just use ChatGPT.

2. **Netflix case study:** In 2008, Netflix had a catastrophic 3-day database failure. That crisis triggered a 7-year data engineering overhaul. Result: systems that now process 500+ billion events per day. That data infrastructure is what powers all Netflix AI/personalization at scale.

3. **Spotify case study:** In 2014, Spotify acquired The Echo Nest for ~$100M — not for an algorithm, but for a database analyzing 30 million songs with over a trillion data points. That became the foundation for Discover Weekly (100B+ streams as of June 2025). Spotify now processes 1.4 trillion data points per day with 100+ engineers on data infrastructure alone.

4. **Why companies fail at AI:** They pour money into ML/AI initiatives, hire engineers, buy third-party tools — then watch it collapse because nobody built the data foundation first. Companies often can't answer basic questions about where their data lives or how it flows.

5. **The three reasons to get into data engineering in 2026:**
   - Competition is low: only 2.5 candidates per job (LinkedIn Data Insights)
   - Pay is serious: avg US salary ~$130K, much higher at top companies
   - Future-proof: more AI = more data, more pipelines, more real-time processing, more governance. You're not being replaced by AI — you're building what AI depends on.

6. **Path into data engineering:** Python + SQL as foundations. Then cloud platforms, Apache Spark, Apache Airflow, Databricks (all listed as "IN DEMAND"). Increasingly: vector databases and real-time data streaming for AI pipelines.

7. **Bridge to AI engineering:** Many AI engineers started as data engineers. The skills overlap significantly. Strong path into AI without needing a PhD.
