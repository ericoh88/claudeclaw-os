# AI Audit as a Service in Singapore
## Legal Compliance, Standards & Market Entry Guide

**Prepared by:** Naventic AI
**Date:** 6 May 2026

## Executive Summary

**Anyone can offer AI audit services in Singapore right now.** There is no license, no mandatory certification, and no law restricting who can do it. Singapore deliberately chose a voluntary, industry-collaborative model. However, sector-specific rules (financial services, personal data) do carry legal teeth, and formal credentialing paths are emerging.

---

## 1. The Legal Landscape

### 1.1 No Mandatory AI Audit Law

Unlike the EU AI Act (which has legally binding conformity assessments with fines up to EUR 35M), Singapore has **no general AI audit requirement**. The entire IMDA framework is voluntary.

### 1.2 PDPA -- The Only Hard Law Touching AI

If your AI system processes personal data, the **Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)** applies:

- **Penalties:** Up to SGD 1 million or 10% of annual turnover (whichever is higher)
- **72-hour data breach notification** is mandatory
- The **PDPC** (Personal Data Protection Commission) now integrates AI governance criteria into PDPA audits
- If you are auditing AI that handles personal data, PDPA compliance is **mandatory, not voluntary**
- Organisations must ensure personal data used in AI training is accurate and complete
- Training AI on inaccurate or biased data can trigger PDPA compliance issues

### 1.3 MAS Guidelines -- Sector-Specific, Effectively Mandatory

The **Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS)** released AI Risk Management Guidelines in November 2025 for all regulated financial institutions:

- Built on **FEAT principles** (Fairness, Ethics, Accountability, Transparency)
- Requires **board-level AI oversight** and three lines of defense
- Full **AI lifecycle controls**: development, deployment, monitoring, retirement
- **Third-party AI tools are covered** -- banks cannot delegate governance to vendors
- This is where AI audit demand is **highest and most urgent** in Singapore

## 2. Can Anyone Do an AI Audit?

### 2.1 Short Answer: Legally, Yes

There is **no license or registration** required to call yourself an AI auditor in Singapore today.

### 2.2 Two Formal Credentialing Paths Are Emerging

#### Path 1: SAC Accreditation (For Certification Bodies)

- **Singapore Accreditation Council (SAC)** launched an **ISO/IEC 42001 accreditation programme on 17 February 2025**
- This is for **certification bodies** (like SGS, BSI, TUV) -- not individual auditors
- Assessed against ISO/IEC 17021-1 and ISO/IEC 42006
- Required to **issue ISO 42001 certificates**

#### Path 2: ISACA AAIA (For Individual Auditors)

- **Advanced in AI Audit (AAIA)** -- first AI audit certification globally
- **Prerequisites:** CISA, CIA, CPA, or ACCA/FCCA + 3 years audit experience (1 year tech-related)
- 90-question exam covering AI governance, risk, and audit methodology
- 10 hours CPE annually in AI domain
- Emerging gold standard for individual AI auditors

## 3. Singapore's AI Governance Framework

### 3.1 Primary Bodies

| Body | Role | Status |
|------|------|--------|
| **IMDA** | Lead agency, Model AI Governance Framework | Voluntary |
| **AI Verify Foundation** | Open-source AI testing toolkit, 90+ members | Voluntary |
| **AI Safety Institute** | Safety testing for high-impact/generative AI | Est. 2025 |
| **National AI Council** | Strategic direction, chaired by PM | Budget 2026 |
| **PDPC** | Personal data protection enforcement | Mandatory |
| **MAS** | Financial services AI risk management | Effectively mandatory |

## 5. Key Differences from EU AI Act

| Dimension | Singapore | EU |
|-----------|-----------|-----|
| Approach | Voluntary frameworks | Prescriptive regulation |
| Risk classification | Self-assessment | Mandatory |
| Penalties | PDPA only (SGD 1M / 10%) | Up to EUR 35M / 7% turnover |
| Audit requirement | None (except MAS) | Mandatory for high-risk |
