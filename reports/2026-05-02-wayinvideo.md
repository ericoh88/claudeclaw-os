# WayinVideo - AI video clipping, summarizing, and repurposing platform

**Site:** https://wayin.ai/
**Chrome Extension:** https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/wayinvideo-ai-youtube-sum/cedgndijpacnfbdggppddacngjfdkaca
**Pricing:** Freemium, starts at $4.99/mo | 200 free credits on signup | No watermarks on paid
**Status:** Active, actively marketed

## What it does

Web-based AI video platform that turns long videos into short clips, summaries, and transcripts. Aimed at creators who want to repurpose long-form content (YouTube, podcasts, lectures) into TikTok/Reels/Shorts without manual editing.

- **AI Clip Maker** - auto-detects highlights, speaker changes, pacing. Generates vertical shorts in one click
- **AI Reframe** - keeps subjects centered when converting aspect ratios (landscape to vertical, etc.)
- **Video Summarizer** - condenses hour-long videos into key points with clickable timestamps
- **Transcription** - accurate transcripts with punctuation, timestamps, speaker labels
- **AI Subtitles** - animated caption styles, callout cards, optimized for social platforms
- **Find Moments** - search by keyword or question to jump to specific scenes
- **100+ languages** supported for summarization and subtitles

## Who it's for

Content creators, YouTubers, social media managers, researchers, educators. Anyone turning long videos into short clips at scale.

## Pros

- Fast long-to-short video repurposing
- No editing skills needed
- Good subtitle/caption styling for social
- Free tier available

## Cons

- AI Reframe needs manual tweaking on multi-subject scenes
- Better for existing video libraries than raw custom uploads
- Web-only, no API or CLI access

## How it compares to existing stack

Overlaps mostly with the "clip long videos into shorts" use case. The main thing it adds is automated highlight detection and one-click vertical reformatting. /watch handles transcription + frame analysis, /video-use does full editing by conversation, and HyperFrames creates from scratch -- so most of WayinVideo's value is already covered, except the auto-highlight detection.
