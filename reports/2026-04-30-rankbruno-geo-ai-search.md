## Summary

Bruno introduces GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) -- the practice of optimizing content so AI search platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude) cite your brand when answering relevant questions. He argues this is a distinct discipline from traditional SEO, not just a rebrand.

## Platform-by-Platform Breakdown

### ChatGPT
- Only 18% of conversations trigger web search
- 82% of responses come from training data and memory
- When it does search, favors authoritative, structured content

### Perplexity
- Always searches the web (real-time retrieval)
- Strong recency bias: 3.5x boost for content under 30 days old
- Most web-dependent of all AI search platforms

### Google AI Overviews
- 76% of citations come from Google's existing top 10
- Traditional SEO performance gives you a head start here
- Most overlap with existing search rankings

### Claude
- Uses Brave Search (not Google)
- Only 20% overlap with ChatGPT's cited sources
- Completely different citation pool -- requires separate optimization

## The GEO Framework (4 Phases)

1. **Content Writing** -- structured, entity-rich content that AI can parse
2. **Technical Foundations** -- schema markup, structured data, machine-readable formats
3. **Entity & Authority** -- third-party mentions, brand signals, being referenced by others
4. **Measurement** -- tracking AI citations and visibility across platforms

## Key Insight

You can't optimize once and expect coverage across all AI platforms. Each has its own retrieval logic, source preferences, and ranking signals. The opportunity is massive because almost nobody is doing this yet.
