# AI Readiness Audit — Full Breakdown

**Date:** 2026-05-06
**Type:** Service Design / Product Definition
**Domain:** AI Governance

## What It Is

A structured assessment that tells an organisation: "Here's where you stand on AI adoption, here are the gaps, and here's what to fix before you deploy AI at scale." It's not about auditing an existing AI system -- it's about assessing whether the organisation is **ready to adopt AI responsibly**.

## Who Needs It

- Companies considering AI adoption but unsure where to start
- Organisations with scattered AI pilots that need to consolidate
- Government agencies preparing for AI procurement
- Companies that want AI but lack governance, data infrastructure, or talent
- Any org that needs to answer: "Are we ready?"

---

## The 7 Pillars of AI Readiness

### Pillar 1: Strategy & Leadership Alignment

**What you assess:**
- Does the organisation have a defined AI strategy?
- Is there executive sponsorship for AI initiatives?
- Is AI aligned with business objectives (not just tech curiosity)?
- Has the board discussed AI governance?
- Is there a roadmap with milestones?

**Deliverables:**
- Strategy maturity score (1-5)
- Gap analysis: strategy vs execution
- Executive alignment heatmap (who's on board, who's not)

**Interview targets:** CEO, CTO, department heads

### Pillar 2: Data Infrastructure & Governance

**What you assess:**
- Data inventory: what data exists, where, in what format
- Data quality: accuracy, completeness, freshness, consistency
- Data governance: ownership, lineage, cataloging
- Data accessibility: silos vs centralised, API availability
- Data security: encryption, access controls, PDPA compliance
- Data volume: enough to train/fine-tune models?

**Deliverables:**
- Data maturity scorecard
- Data quality report (sample audit of 3-5 key datasets)
- Data governance gap analysis
- PDPA compliance checklist for AI use cases

**Tools used:** SQL profiling, data quality frameworks, schema analysis

### Pillar 3: Technology Infrastructure

**What you assess:**
- Compute capacity: on-prem GPUs, cloud GPU access, budget
- MLOps maturity: CI/CD for models, experiment tracking, model registry
- Integration readiness: APIs, middleware, data pipelines
- Cloud vs on-prem posture
- Existing AI/ML tools and platforms in use
- Vendor lock-in risk

**Deliverables:**
- Infrastructure readiness score
- Architecture diagram (current state vs target state)
- Cost estimate for required upgrades
- Vendor/platform recommendations

**PTC strength:** They already assess infrastructure. Add the AI lens on top.

### Pillar 4: Talent & Skills

**What you assess:**
- Current AI/ML headcount and capability
- Data engineering and data science maturity
- AI literacy across the organisation (not just tech teams)
- Training programmes in place or planned
- Ability to attract and retain AI talent
- External partner/vendor dependency

**Deliverables:**
- Skills gap matrix (role x capability)
- Training roadmap
- Build vs buy vs partner recommendation
- Organisational design suggestions (where does the AI team sit?)

**Interview targets:** HR, engineering leads, data teams

### Pillar 5: Use Case Identification & Prioritisation

**What you assess:**
- Inventory of proposed AI use cases
- Feasibility scoring: data availability, technical complexity, business impact
- Risk classification per use case (IMDA / EU AI Act risk tiers)
- Quick wins vs long-term bets
- ROI estimation per use case
- Dependencies between use cases

**Deliverables:**
- Prioritised use case matrix (impact vs feasibility)
- Top 3-5 recommended pilot use cases with justification
- Risk classification per use case
- Estimated timeline and budget per use case

**Framework:** 2x2 matrix -- Business Impact (high/low) x Implementation Feasibility (high/low). Top-right quadrant is pilot shortlist.

### Pillar 6: Governance, Ethics & Risk

**What you assess:**
- Existing AI governance policies (if any)
- Awareness of IMDA Model AI Governance Framework
- Ethical AI principles adoption
- Bias and fairness considerations
- Transparency and explainability requirements
- Human oversight mechanisms
- Incident response planning for AI failures
- Third-party AI vendor governance

**Deliverables:**
- Governance maturity assessment (mapped to IMDA framework)
- AI ethics policy template (customised)
- Risk register template for AI initiatives
- Recommended governance structure (AI committee, RACI)

### Pillar 7: Change Management & Culture

**What you assess:**
- Organisational appetite for AI (enthusiasm vs resistance)
- Communication plan for AI adoption
- Stakeholder mapping (champions, blockers, neutral)
- Impact on existing roles and workflows
- Union/employee relations considerations
- Customer communication readiness

**Deliverables:**
- Change readiness score
- Stakeholder influence map
- Communication plan template
- Transition plan for affected roles

## Audit Delivery Structure

| Phase | Duration | Activities |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Kickoff | 1 week | Scope agreement, document collection, stakeholder mapping |
| Discovery | 2-3 weeks | Interviews (8-12 stakeholders), data assessment, infra review, use case workshops |
| Analysis | 1-2 weeks | Scoring, gap analysis, recommendations, report writing |
| Presentation | 1 day | Executive briefing + detailed findings + roadmap |
| Follow-up | 1 week | Q&A, refinements, handover |

**Total: 5-7 weeks**

---

## Scoring Model

Each pillar scored 1-5:

| Score | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Ad Hoc | No formal approach, individual efforts |
| 2 | Aware | Some awareness, no structured programme |
| 3 | Developing | Policies and processes being established |
| 4 | Managed | Systematic approach, metrics in place |
| 5 | Optimised | Continuous improvement, industry-leading |

**Overall AI Readiness Score** = weighted average across 7 pillars

## Deliverables Package

1. Executive Summary (2-3 pages) -- for the board

3. AI Readiness Scorecard -- radar chart + scores per pillar

5. Gap Analysis & Recommendations -- what to fix, in what order
6. AI Governance Starter Kit -- policy templates, RACI, risk register
