# WayinVideo - Features, Benefits & Pricing Summary

**Site:** https://wayin.ai/
**Date:** 2026-05-02

## Features

- **AI Clip Maker** - auto-detects highlights from long videos, generates vertical shorts in one click
- **AI Reframe** - keeps subjects centered when switching aspect ratios (landscape to vertical, etc.)
- **Video Summarizer** - condenses hour-long videos into key points with clickable timestamps
- **Transcription** - accurate transcripts with punctuation, timestamps, speaker labels
- **AI Subtitles** - animated caption styles, callout cards, optimized for TikTok/Reels
- **Find Moments** - search video by keyword or question, jump to exact scene instantly
- **100+ languages** supported for summarization and subtitles

## How You Benefit

- Turn one long YouTube video or podcast into 5-10 short clips without editing
- Auto-vertical formatting saves manual crop/reframe work
- Subtitle styling built-in, no ffmpeg subtitle burning needed
- Keyword search across video content means you can find "that one thing someone said" instantly
- Good for influencer monitoring workflow -- when a new video drops, clip the best moments fast
- No editing skills needed, fully web-based

## Pricing

- **Free tier** - basic features, limited credits
- **200 free credits** on signup, no watermark
- **Paid plans** start at **$4.99/month**
- Periodic sales up to **65% off**

## Honest Take

Most of WayinVideo's value is already covered by the existing /watch + /video-use + HyperFrames stack. The one thing it adds that the current setup doesn't have is automated highlight detection -- it picks the viral-worthy moments for you instead of you watching and choosing manually. At $5/month it's cheap enough to try alongside existing tools.
