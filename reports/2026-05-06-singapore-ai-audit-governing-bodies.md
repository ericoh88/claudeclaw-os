# AI Audit Governing Bodies in Singapore

**Date:** 2026-05-06
**Type:** Research / Regulatory Landscape
**Domain:** AI Governance

## Overview

There is no single "AI audit regulator" in Singapore. Instead, it's a layered, mostly voluntary system led by IMDA and several subsidiary bodies.

## Primary Bodies

### IMDA (Infocomm Media Development Authority)
- Lead agency for AI governance in Singapore
- Publishes the Model AI Governance Framework, including the newest framework for Agentic AI (January 2026)
- Compliance is voluntary, but organisations are legally liable for their AI systems' actions
- Website: https://www.imda.gov.sg/about-imda/emerging-technologies-and-research/artificial-intelligence

### AI Verify Foundation
- Not-for-profit subsidiary of IMDA
- Maintains the open-source AI Verify testing toolkit
- Validates AI across: transparency, fairness, safety, robustness, explainability, data governance
- 90+ member organisations
- Closest thing to a standardised AI audit tool in Singapore

### AI Safety Institute
- Established 2025 under IMDA
- Conducts safety and alignment testing for high-impact and generative AI models
- Works in tandem with AI Verify Foundation on evaluation protocols

### National AI Council
- Announced in Budget 2026, chaired by the Prime Minister
- Strategic direction, not operational auditing

## Certification Landscape

- IMDA works with accredited third-party certification bodies (independent auditors)
- No mandatory AI audit certification yet
- Framework supports both self-assessment and third-party audits
- An AI Assurance Framework unifying technical, organisational, and ethical testing is planned for 2026

## Key Difference from EU

Singapore deliberately chose a voluntary, industry-collaborative model rather than prescriptive regulation like the EU AI Act. There is no mandatory risk classification or conformity assessment -- yet.

## Implications for Naventic AI

- Position AI audit offering around AI Verify as the testing backbone
- Reference IMDA governance framework for structural methodology
- Offer third-party audit services -- clear demand but limited supply of qualified auditors in the region
- Monitor the upcoming AI Assurance Framework for potential mandatory requirements
