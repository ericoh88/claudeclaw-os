AI Native Engineer Roadmap

How to Use This Roadmap

  1. Make a copy of this document so you can check off items as you go.
  2. Set a weekly calendar reminder for the next 12 weeks to review your progress and plan the upcoming week.
  3. The Q&A sessions will keep you accountable. Come prepared to discuss what you completed and where you’re stuck.
  4. Engage with the community throughout the program. Ask questions, share wins, and connect with other engineers on the same journey.

The Program Philosophy

You’re not here to collect course certificates. You’re here to become a competent AI Engineer.

This program runs on two parallel tracks:

  1. Reach: Your LinkedIn presence compounds over time. We start this immediately so by Week 12, you have 10+ weeks of activity behind you. This is your unfair advantage. Your weekly LinkedIn posts should document what you’re learning: new concepts, breakthroughs, mistakes, and insights from the modules.
  2. Skills: You’ll build 1-3 AI projects throughout the program to develop real skills. But only ONE capstone project matters for your job search: the one you’ll showcase on LinkedIn and talk about in interviews.

If you joined the community purely to gain AI skills, then you can generally ignore the LinkedIn directives

Of course, if time permits, feel free to work on multiple weeks at the same time. In any case, by following this template, you can finish the core community content guaranteed in 12 weeks.

Timeline Overview

  • Onboarding (Week 1): Community setup, goal alignment
  • LinkedIn (Week 2): Profile, engagement, content system
  • Technical (Weeks 3-9): AI engineering skills, practice projects, capstone build
  • Career (Weeks 10-12): Resume polish, mock interviews, job applications

Key Milestones

  • Milestone 1 (Week 2): LinkedIn optimized, first post published
  • Milestone 2 (Week 6): Core technical skills acquired, practice projects complete
  • Milestone 3 (Week 9): Capstone project demo-ready
  • Milestone 4 (Week 12): Resume polished, 10+ applications submitted, interview pipeline started
